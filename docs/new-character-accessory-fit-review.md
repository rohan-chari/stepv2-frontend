## Manual checklist

1. TestFlight → Shop → Characters → character → Edit. Preview each accessory individually and watch a full loop, including unowned items. Record keep/remove/adjust below.
2. Check location against the body, clipping, duplicate overlays and previous selection cleanup. Pay attention to mouse ears, sea lion chest, otter tail and hedgehog quills.
3. For owned accessories, save an outfit and check Home, races, available team slots, leaderboard and public profile at smaller sizes on both supported test-channel platforms.
4. Demo/tab tutorials have separate fixtures and do not validate these combinations. Unavailable states remain untested.
