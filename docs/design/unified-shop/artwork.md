# Coin sack artwork

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool from the approved [concept](coin-sacks-concept.png). Final transparent PNGs are bundled in `assets/images/shop/`. Three separate generations; no hand-drawn artwork. Preview on white and green at 100px verified the silhouette, black keyline, plain coins, and increasing spill. Editable Aseprite sources and targeted export cases are installed in the local art repository (paths in CLAUDE.local.md). Original generated PNGs are retained at the tool output location.

## small

Asset: `assets/images/shop/coin_sack_small.png`

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: final transparent PNG game shop product illustration. Reference image is an approved concept sheet; extract/recreate ONLY the small sack described, not the full sheet. SMALL 500-coin pack: a small tan burlap pouch, modest narrow open top with just 3 to 5 plain gold coins visible, no surrounding pile. Match leftmost reference sack. Center the single isolated object in square canvas with 10% transparent padding. Preserve the reference's chunky retro pixel art with visible large square pixels, strong continuous black outlines, warm tan/brown sack and golden yellow coins, stepped soft shading. Three-quarter view, crisp silhouette readable at 100px display. No text, price, number, paw print, symbols, labels or watermark. Genuine transparent background/alpha, no white or checkerboard background baked in, no cast background shadow. No extra objects or characters. This is not wearable art. Match the reference family faithfully.
